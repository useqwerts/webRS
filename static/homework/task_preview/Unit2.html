Unit 1: Defining and Non-defining Relative Clauses

There are two types of relative clauses: defining and non-defining. In both types, the relative clause gives information about a person or thing...

Defining relative clauses

  1. I want to meet the person who found my handbag.
  2. It’s the gadget that is used for opening cans.
  3. Isn’t that the woman whose handbag you found?
